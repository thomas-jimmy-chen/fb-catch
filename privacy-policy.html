FB Catch — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

TL;DR: FB Catch processes everything locally in your browser. No data is collected, stored on external servers, or shared with anyone. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics.

1. What FB Catch Does

FB Catch is a Chrome extension that lets you export posts, comments, reactions, and media from Facebook Pages you visit. It works entirely within your browser — there is no backend server, cloud service, or external API involved.

2. Data We Collect

None.

FB Catch does not collect, transmit, or store any user data on external servers. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected
  • No browsing history is tracked
  • No analytics or telemetry is sent
  • No cookies are read or transmitted to third parties
  • No account or login is required

3. Data That Stays on Your Device

All data processed by FB Catch remains entirely on your local machine:

DataStoragePurpose
Exported posts & commentsBrowser downloads folderUser-requested export
Downloaded images & videosBrowser downloads folderUser-requested media download
Extension settingschrome.storage.localRemember user preferences
Scan progress & logschrome.storage.localResume interrupted scans

chrome.storage.local is a browser-managed storage area accessible only to this extension. It does not sync across devices. This data may be cleared if you clear your browser data or remove the extension.

You can clear all stored data at any time by removing the extension or going to chrome://extensions → FB Catch → Details → Clear Storage.

4. Permissions Explained

FB Catch requests the following browser permissions, each for a specific purpose:

PermissionWhy It's Needed
storageSave your settings and scan progress locally
scriptingInject the export interface into Facebook pages
downloadsSave exported files (JSON, CSV, images) to your computer
tabsDetect which Facebook page you're on; navigate during batch export
webNavigationMonitor page load completion during batch export
offscreenCreate temporary download URLs for exported files (Service Workers cannot create blob URLs directly)
host_permissions (facebook.com, fb.com)Access Facebook pages to read publicly visible content
host_permissions (fbcdn.net)Download images and videos from Facebook's content delivery network

No permission is used to collect, transmit, or share any data with external parties.

5. Third-Party Services

FB Catch does not use any third-party services, including:

  • No analytics (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.)
  • No crash reporting
  • No advertising
  • No external APIs
  • No CDN or cloud storage

6. Limited Use Disclosure

FB Catch's use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Data obtained through Chrome APIs is used solely to provide the extension's stated functionality (exporting posts, comments, and media)
  • Data is not transferred to any external server, third party, or advertising service
  • Data is not used for purposes unrelated to the extension's core functionality
  • Data is not sold, shared, or used for creditworthiness determination or lending purposes

7. Facebook Data

FB Catch only accesses publicly available content that you can already see in your browser. The extension does not:

  • Access private messages or non-public content
  • Bypass any access controls or privacy settings
  • Store or transmit your Facebook credentials
  • Perform any actions on your behalf (no posting, liking, or following)

Media downloads (images and videos) are initiated solely by user action and are saved directly to the user's local device. FB Catch is designed for users to back up their own content; users are responsible for ensuring they have the right to download and store any media they export.

This is an independent tool, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Meta Platforms, Inc. Users are solely responsible for ensuring their use of this extension complies with Facebook's Terms of Service, applicable laws, and local regulations. FB Catch does not bypass access controls, but users remain accountable for how they use exported data.

8. Children's Privacy

FB Catch is not designed for, targeted at, or intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you are under 13, please do not use this extension.

9. Regional Privacy Rights

European Union (GDPR)

FB Catch does not process any personal data as defined by the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). All data processing occurs locally on your device and never leaves your browser. Since no personal data is collected or transmitted externally, there is no data controller, no data processing agreement required, and no Data Protection Officer designated.

California (CCPA/CPRA)

FB Catch does not sell, share, or disclose any personal information as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act. All data remains under your local control.

Taiwan (PDPA)

FB Catch does not collect, process, or use personal data as defined by the Taiwan Personal Data Protection Act. No data is transmitted to any party.

10. Data Retention

FB Catch does not retain any data on external servers. Local data stored in chrome.storage.local persists until:

  • You uninstall the extension (all data is automatically deleted)
  • You manually clear the extension's storage
  • You clear your browser's local data

Downloaded files (JSON, CSV, images, videos) remain in your downloads folder and are managed by your operating system, not by the extension.

11. Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top will be revised. Since FB Catch collects no data, meaningful changes are unlikely. Material changes will be announced via the extension's GitHub repository.
